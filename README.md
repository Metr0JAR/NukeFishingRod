Turn your boring fishing rod into an explosive nuke launcher! 🎣💣 Cast your nearly broken fishing rod, and watch 50 TNT blocks rain from the sky in a square! 🌩️💥

The plugin adds glow to show your rod is ready to unleash chaos. Once used, the rod breaks instantly, making it a one-time-use weapon of mass destruction! ⚡💀

Test it easily with /getnukerod 🛠️ to get a nearly broken rod and prepare for epic TNT fun! 🎉
To use nuke just do /nuke on your 0 durability fishingrod and right click.
